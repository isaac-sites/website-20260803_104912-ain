## Introduction

Two Belfort sightings, in 2018 and 2020, show how an ordinary kite can be interpreted as a hovering aircraft when distance, scale and attachment to the ground are not visible. The first report attracted several witnesses and photographs of a dark rectangular object apparently remaining above the city for about half an hour. Suggestions included a drone, a balloon and even an experimental solar aircraft. Within a day, however, local information pointed to a large kite flown from the Pépinière district.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.estrepublicain.fr/edition-belfort-hericourt-montbeliard/2018/07/31/un-ovni-dans-le-ciel-belfortain" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: estrepublicain.fr">[L&#x27;Est Républicain]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">estrepublicain.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-title">L&#x27;Est Républicain Belfort</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Un Ovni dans le ciel belfortain?July 31, 2018 — 31 Jul 2018 — Ce n&#x27;est visiblement ni une montgolfière, ni un cerf-volant, ni un ballon...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 31, 2018</span></span></span>

The 2020 observation was more formally documented. A witness supplied a detailed account, a photograph, estimated directions and a drawing to GEIPAN, the French space agency’s investigation group. ## The 2018 object that looked unlike any aircraft

On the evening of Saturday 28 July 2018, several Belfort residents noticed a dark object above the city. Early reporting described it as rectangular, surrounded by an orange-looking glow and apparently stationary for more than 30 minutes. Some witnesses believed the colour changed shortly before the object disappeared from view.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.estrepublicain.fr/edition-belfort-hericourt-montbeliard/2018/07/31/un-ovni-dans-le-ciel-belfortain" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: estrepublicain.fr">[L&#x27;Est Républicain]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">estrepublicain.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-title">L&#x27;Est Républicain Belfort</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Un Ovni dans le ciel belfortain?July 31, 2018 — 31 Jul 2018 — Ce n&#x27;est visiblement ni une montgolfière, ni un cerf-volant, ni un ballon...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 31, 2018</span></span></span>

Those details encouraged comparisons with aircraft even though the reported behaviour did not fit an ordinary aeroplane. The object seemed to hover, made no obvious rapid crossing of the sky and had no recognisable wings or fuselage. A witness suggested that it might be a solar-aircraft prototype, while the newspaper also raised the increasingly familiar possibility of a sophisticated drone. The original article explicitly treated a kite as an unlikely option, illustrating how an explanation can be dismissed when observers imagine only a small, familiar toy rather than a large specialist model.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.estrepublicain.fr/edition-belfort-hericourt-montbeliard/2018/07/31/un-ovni-dans-le-ciel-belfortain" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: estrepublicain.fr">[L&#x27;Est Républicain]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">estrepublicain.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-title">L&#x27;Est Républicain Belfort</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Un Ovni dans le ciel belfortain?July 31, 2018 — 31 Jul 2018 — Ce n&#x27;est visiblement ni une montgolfière, ni un cerf-volant, ni un ballon...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 31, 2018</span></span></span>

The apparent mystery weakened almost immediately. Readers familiar with the object reported that it was a large kite operated from the Pépinière area of Belfort. It had reportedly been seen repeatedly since the beginning of July and, according to local accounts, similar flights had taken place there for several years. The conspicuous brightness was attributed to reflected light from a GoPro camera carried by the kite rather than to lamps or an aircraft propulsion system.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.estrepublicain.fr/edition-belfort-hericourt-montbeliard/2018/08/01/l-ovni-serait-en-fait-un-cerf-volant" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: estrepublicain.fr">[L&#x27;Est Républicain]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">estrepublicain.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-title">L&#x27;Est Républicain Insolite</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">L&#x27;Ovni qui survolait Belfort? C&#x27;était un cerf-volant1 Aug 2018 — Qu&#x27;était donc cet engin qui a survolé le ciel belfortain dans la soirée...</span></span></span>

That first explanation was cautiously worded in the press as the “most plausible” identification rather than the conclusion of a technical investigation. Later material considerably strengthened it. GEIPAN’s subsequent review located films showing the object, identified a cable holding it in position and found that the filmed views were consistent with the Pépinière sector. Its report also noted further observations in August 2018, including one lasting approximately two hours, and statements from residents who confirmed that the object was a kite.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cnes-geipan.fr/sites/default/files/Compte%20rendu%20enquete635.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cnes-geipan.fr">[GEIPAN]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cnes-geipan.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Compte rendu enquete635</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">GEIPANModèle de document par défaut CNES version 2.0 Janvier 2010...</span></span></span>

The long duration was therefore not evidence against a kite. It was one of the most useful clues in its favour. A tethered object can occupy roughly the same part of the sky for a prolonged period while shifting, twisting or rising slightly in the wind. A powered aircraft could hover, but doing so over an urban area for half an hour would normally introduce other clues: engine or rotor noise, purposeful manoeuvres, navigation lights or a clear departure. None was securely established in the 2018 accounts.

## Why witnesses and photographs still gave the wrong impression

The Belfort case is not best understood as careless observation. The witnesses noticed real features: a dark shape, apparent immobility, changing orientation and unusual brightness. The mistake occurred when those features were combined into an assumed aircraft rather than recognised as parts of a kite system.

The central problem was **unknown distance**. When an object has no familiar size and is seen against open sky, an observer cannot reliably determine how large or distant it is. A relatively nearby kite may therefore be experienced as a much larger craft farther away. Estimates of altitude, separation between components and physical dimensions can appear precise while depending on an untested assumption about distance.

The object’s outline added to the confusion. Large kites need not have the diamond shape associated with children’s toys. Specialist designs can be rectangular, inflated, sculptural or almost animal-like. In November 2020, for example, a Belfort enthusiast flew a 32-square-metre kite shaped like a trilobite from the Pépinière area using 200 metres of line. That was a different documented flight, but it demonstrates the size and visual unfamiliarity that local kite activity could produce.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.estrepublicain.fr/insolite/2020/11/08/un-trilobite-dans-le-ciel-de-la-pepiniere" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: estrepublicain.fr">[L&#x27;Est Républicain]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">estrepublicain.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-title">L&#x27;Est Républicain Belfort</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Un trilobite dans le ciel de la PépinièreNovember 8, 2020 — 8 Nov 2020 — Denis, Belfortain passionné de cerfs-volants depuis trente ans...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 8, 2020</span></span></span>

Photography did not automatically resolve the uncertainty. The 2018 images established that something was present, but a small object occupying few pixels supplied little dependable information about its construction or distance. A reflective camera housing could appear as a halo, while atmospheric haze, exposure and digital enlargement could soften the kite’s edges. Photographs are valuable evidence, but they are not self-interpreting; they become decisive only when matched with location, shape, movement and an independently known object.

Multiple witnesses also increased confidence without necessarily improving identification. People viewing the same unfamiliar object from the same general area share many of the same limitations. Several reports can strongly confirm that an observation occurred, yet still reproduce a common error about scale or nature. GEIPAN itself distinguishes the quality of a case’s documentation from its level of strangeness: a well-described incident may be convincingly explained, while a poorly documented one may remain unresolved merely because essential information is missing.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cnes-geipan.fr/sites/default/files/News-V3-VBA-February20-2018_V1.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cnes-geipan.fr">[GEIPAN]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cnes-geipan.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-title">GEIPANMissions, Methods and Achievements</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">July 25, 2019 — One or several witnesses are the starting point of a GEIPAN case. ## The 2020 sighting recreated the same illusion

At about 4.45–5.00 pm on 21 January 2020, a Belfort resident looking from a sixth-floor flat saw a dark object apparently suspended in the sky. Below it were four elongated, ribbon-like forms in different colours—remembered as red, green, blue and possibly yellow. The upper object seemed to rotate slowly around a vertical axis but remained in the same general direction until fading daylight prevented further observation, roughly 30 minutes later.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.geipan.fr/en/node/60427?field_classification_des_cas_target_id%5B0%5D=11&amp;page=%2C30" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: geipan.fr">[Geipan]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">geipan.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Le cas est de bonne consistance: présence d’une photo et très bonne qualité du témoignage (voir le compte rendu d&#x27;enquête).En cherchant...</span></span></span>

The witness’s drawing preserved that perception as five distinct phenomena: one dark body and four coloured bands.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cnes-geipan.fr/sites/default/files/Compte%20rendu%20enquete635.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cnes-geipan.fr">[GEIPAN]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cnes-geipan.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Compte rendu enquete635</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">GEIPANModèle de document par défaut CNES version 2.0 Janvier 2010...</span></span></span>

## How later evidence changed the strength of the claim

The two episodes did not have identical evidential histories. In 2018, the story moved quickly from a striking newspaper report to a locally informed explanation. At that stage, the kite conclusion was persuasive but was presented through readers’ responses and knowledge of repeated flights rather than a published official reconstruction.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.estrepublicain.fr/edition-belfort-hericourt-montbeliard/2018/07/31/un-ovni-dans-le-ciel-belfortain" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: estrepublicain.fr">[L&#x27;Est Républicain]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">estrepublicain.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-title">L&#x27;Est Républicain Belfort</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Un Ovni dans le ciel belfortain?July 31, 2018 — 31 Jul 2018 — Ce n&#x27;est visiblement ni une montgolfière, ni un cerf-volant, ni un ballon...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 31, 2018</span></span></span>

The 2020 submission prompted GEIPAN to assemble the earlier material into a more complete case history. Investigators compared photographs and video frames, considered the viewing direction, noted the stationary cable-supported behaviour and collected accounts of repeated sightings. The later report recorded that one 2018 film showed the object attached to a line and that another image contained a recognisable chimney useful for matching the scene.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cnes-geipan.fr/sites/default/files/Compte%20rendu%20enquete635.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cnes-geipan.fr">[GEIPAN]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cnes-geipan.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Compte rendu enquete635</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">GEIPANModèle de document par défaut CNES version 2.0 Janvier 2010...</span></span></span>

This retrospective work strengthened the 2018 identification rather than reopening the mystery. Features originally presented as evidence of an unusual aircraft—hovering, silence, an unfamiliar outline and an apparent luminous effect—became mutually supporting features of the kite explanation:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--benefit" markdown="1">

* **Hovering:** expected for a tethered kite held in a stable airflow.
* **Slow rotation:** consistent with a kite shifting around its line.
* **Coloured lower objects:** consistent with tails, streamers or suspended elements.
* **The “halo”:** plausibly produced by light reflecting from the camera.
* **Repeated appearances in one sector:** consistent with a local operator using the same flying site.
* **No obvious departure:** the object was lost in changing light or ceased to be watched rather than being observed accelerating away.

</div>

One detail should nevertheless remain carefully phrased. The evidence securely identifies a kite, but not every visual effect can be reconstructed with laboratory precision from the available public records. The 2018 report of changing colours, for example, may have involved reflection, orientation, atmosphere or interpretation. It is unnecessary to settle each minor impression separately once the object itself has been independently recognised.

## Belfort’s clearest lesson in mistaken aerial perception

These cases matter within the Territoire de Belfort’s UFO history because they preserve both stages of an identification: the compelling first impression and the later conventional answer. They are stronger educational examples than a report dismissed merely because it sounds vague. The 2020 witness was observant, sceptically minded and methodical; GEIPAN considered his account and photograph good-quality evidence. He was mistaken not about seeing an object, but about how high, distant and aircraft-like it appeared.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cnes-geipan.fr/sites/default/files/Compte%20rendu%20enquete635.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cnes-geipan.fr">[GEIPAN]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cnes-geipan.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Compte rendu enquete635</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">GEIPANModèle de document par défaut CNES version 2.0 Janvier 2010...</span></span></span>

The case family also shows why “unidentified” should describe the state of knowledge at a particular time, not the inherent nature of an object. On the evening of the observation, the kite was genuinely unidentified to the witnesses. After comparison with local footage, repeated sightings and the known Pépinière flights, the uncertainty disappeared.

For the department’s wider record, the Belfort kite sightings therefore weigh against claims of a recurring unknown aircraft. Later reporting did not reveal military tests, unusual aviation activity or unexplained technology. It connected two apparently separate episodes to a persistent and visually distinctive local kite. The result is not evidence that every strange aerial report has an easy answer, but a precise demonstration of how strong ordinary explanations emerge when photographs, geography, repeated observations and local knowledge are brought together.
