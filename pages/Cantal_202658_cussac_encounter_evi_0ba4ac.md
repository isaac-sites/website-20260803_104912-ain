## Introduction

The Cussac encounter began on the morning of 29 August 1967, when two children tending cattle outside the [Cantal]({{ 'cantals-ufo-story-beyond-the-mystery/' | relative_url }}) village reported four small dark figures beside a brilliant rounded object. They said the figures rose or leapt into it before it departed with a whistle, intense light and a brief strange smell. The children returned home badly frightened, and gendarmes visited the site later that day.



<img src="{{ "/assets/images/Cantal_202658_cussac_encounter_evi_0ba4ac-overview.webp" | relative_url }}" alt="Overview image for Cussac" loading="eager" decoding="sync" fetchpriority="high">

That core story is well established. The harder question is what it proves. Cussac has no photograph, radar record, recoverable material or securely preserved contemporary police file. Its strongest evidence is the apparent sincerity and immediate distress of two young witnesses. Its weaknesses lie in viewing distance, a very brief observation, uncertain physical traces and details that were expanded, altered or challenged during investigations conducted years later. The result is neither a confirmed extraordinary event nor an easy hoax: it is Cantal’s defining unresolved witness case, but one whose evidential value has often been overstated.

## The children’s original account

François, aged thirteen, and his nine-year-old sister Anne-Marie were watching the family’s cattle near a road outside Cussac at about 10.30 in the morning. François’s attention was reportedly drawn towards a pasture across the road when the family dog reacted to the herd. The children then noticed several dark figures near a bright object partly framed by a hedge.

In the account that circulated soon afterwards, the figures were roughly child-sized but oddly proportioned and completely dark. François initially seems to have wondered whether they were unfamiliar children. He and his sister then described movements that appeared impossible or at least highly unusual: the figures rose from the ground and entered, or seemed to be absorbed into, a shining sphere. One figure allegedly returned briefly before following the others. The object then climbed away, becoming too bright to watch. The whole principal observation may have lasted only about thirty seconds.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://gregorygutierez.com/doku.php/ovni/l_obversation_de_cussac_en_1968_historique_des_debats?do=export_pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gregorygutierez.com">[gregorygutierez.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gregorygutierez.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">l obversation de cussac en 1968 historique des debats</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">L&#x27;observation de Cussac en 1967: historique des débats...</span></span></span>

The reported distance matters. Later reconstructions commonly placed the object and figures around 70 to 80 metres from the children, rather than at truly close conversational range. A hedge stood near or between the witnesses and the reported scene, reducing confidence in fine details close to the ground. This makes descriptions such as webbed feet, supporting legs beneath the sphere or small handheld objects much less secure than the broader impressions of dark figures and a bright rounded form.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://gregorygutierez.com/doku.php/ovni/l_obversation_de_cussac_en_1968_historique_des_debats?do=export_pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gregorygutierez.com">[gregorygutierez.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gregorygutierez.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">l obversation de cussac en 1968 historique des debats</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">L&#x27;observation de Cussac en 1967: historique des débats...</span></span></span>

The children’s reaction is one of the case’s better-supported features. A farmer whom they encountered soon afterwards recalled that they appeared panicked and asked whether he had seen or heard anything. Their father also saw them return home crying. Later accounts described continuing shock, disturbed sleep and eye irritation in François. These observations support the conclusion that the children were genuinely frightened; they do not identify the cause of that fear.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://gregorygutierez.com/doku.php/ovni/l_obversation_de_cussac_en_1968_historique_des_debats?do=export_pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gregorygutierez.com">[gregorygutierez.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gregorygutierez.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">l obversation de cussac en 1968 historique des debats</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">L&#x27;observation de Cussac en 1967: historique des débats...</span></span></span>

### What was reported immediately

The central narrative entered public circulation quickly. Radio Luxembourg reportedly covered the story the following day, and the regional newspaper *La Montagne* interviewed the family for an article published on 1 September. Other newspapers and specialist UFO publications followed. That short interval limits the possibility that the entire incident was invented years afterwards, but it does not mean every familiar detail appeared in the earliest version.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.zetetique.fr/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/OvniDuCnes_chapitre10.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: zetetique.fr">[Observatoire zététique]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">zetetique.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Observatoire zététique</span></span></span>

The stable elements appear to have been:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* two frightened child witnesses;<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.researchgate.net/publication/341443875_Aliens_and_Unidentified_Aerial_Phenomena" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: researchgate.net">[researchgate.net]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">researchgate.net</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Source details in endnotes.</span></span></span>
* four dark, small-looking figures;
* a conspicuous rounded or spherical object;
* a rapid departure involving bright light and a whistling or rushing sound;
* disturbance among the dog and cattle.

</div>

More elaborate features are harder to date securely. Webbed feet, an object like a mirror in one figure’s hand, landing supports, a spiral ascent and pronounced heat effects appear most clearly in later published or reconstructed accounts. Some may reflect genuine recollection. Others may have been sharpened through questioning, illustration and repeated retelling.

This distinction is central to Cussac. A report can remain consistent in its broad outline while becoming progressively more detailed. Greater detail does not automatically mean stronger evidence, especially when it emerges after witnesses have seen their experience represented in newspapers, drawings and UFO literature.

## The gendarmerie visit and the missing record

The children’s father, who was also the village mayor, contacted the authorities. Gendarmes from the Saint-Flour and Neuvéglise area reportedly arrived at approximately 4pm, several hours after the sighting. They examined the location and spoke to those involved. Later summaries say they noticed an unusual smell and a patch of yellowed or dried grass.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.zetetique.fr/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/OvniDuCnes_chapitre10.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: zetetique.fr">[Observatoire zététique]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">zetetique.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Observatoire zététique</span></span></span>

This official response is often presented as if it supplied independent physical confirmation. In reality, its value is limited by a serious archival problem: no contemporary written gendarmerie statement or site report has been securely produced as the foundational document of the case. Later GEPAN material reportedly stated that the officers had not considered it necessary to prepare a formal record. What survives is therefore mainly retrospective information about what the gendarmes were said to have observed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.zetetique.fr/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/OvniDuCnes_chapitre10.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: zetetique.fr">[Observatoire zététique]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">zetetique.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Observatoire zététique</span></span></span>

That difference is important. A dated police report could establish precisely:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* who inspected the site;
* what was present when they arrived;
* the dimensions and position of any grass discoloration;
* whether the smell was detected independently;
* what the children said before extensive media attention;
* what ordinary aircraft or local activity were checked.

</div>

Without such a document, later claims about “officially confirmed traces” should be treated cautiously.

The grass evidence is particularly unstable. Early UFO writing described a circular landing trace. Yet François reportedly told investigators in 1983 that he had never personally seen a trace in the field. Other accounts refer only to a four- or five-metre area of yellowed grass, without soil sampling, photographs, measurements or proof that the discoloration appeared after the sighting.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://gregorygutierez.com/doku.php/ovni/l_obversation_de_cussac_en_1968_historique_des_debats?do=export_pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gregorygutierez.com">[gregorygutierez.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gregorygutierez.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">l obversation de cussac en 1968 historique des debats</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">L&#x27;observation de Cussac en 1967: historique des débats...</span></span></span>

A smell described as sulphur, ozone or something similarly sharp was also reported. François later characterised it as fleeting, while the village field guard said he noticed an odour only when visiting the pasture that evening. Smell is real evidence in the everyday sense, but it is difficult to preserve or identify retrospectively. It could be compatible with exhaust, disturbed vegetation, agricultural material or another local source as readily as with an unknown machine.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://gregorygutierez.com/doku.php/ovni/l_obversation_de_cussac_en_1968_historique_des_debats?do=export_pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gregorygutierez.com">[gregorygutierez.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gregorygutierez.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">l obversation de cussac en 1968 historique des debats</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">L&#x27;observation de Cussac en 1967: historique des débats...</span></span></span>

## How later investigations changed the story

Cussac was repeatedly investigated, but the most influential inquiry occurred long after the event. France’s National Centre for Space Studies created GEPAN, its first official unit for studying unidentified aerospace phenomena, in 1977. In 1978 Claude Poher and three colleagues travelled to Cussac, interviewed the witnesses, measured the terrain and attempted a reconstruction—roughly eleven years after the original sighting.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cnes-geipan.fr/fr/node/438" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cnes-geipan.fr">[GEIPAN]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cnes-geipan.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">depuis 1977, date de la création du GEPAN (Groupe d’Étude des Phénomènes Aérospatiaux Non identifiés).... Cette recommandation se retrou...</span></span></span>

The team reached a striking conclusion. Its internal report stated that the witnesses had seen the hurried departure of an inhabited, unknown vehicle with characteristics beyond the investigators’ technological knowledge. The report was not initially published, reportedly following the advice of GEPAN’s scientific council, and the witnesses themselves were not told its conclusion.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.zetetique.fr/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/OvniDuCnes_chapitre10.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: zetetique.fr">[Observatoire zététique]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">zetetique.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Observatoire zététique</span></span></span>

GEPAN’s work strengthened the case in several ways. Investigators returned to the location, interviewed both witnesses and sought to test competing descriptions against the geography. They also used smell samples and considered whether a helicopter could fit the account. Later pro-UFO summaries, including the COMETA report, cited Cussac as a case whose main description had been reinforced by official reinvestigation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://gregorygutierez.com/doku.php/ovni/l_obversation_de_cussac_en_1968_historique_des_debats?do=export_pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gregorygutierez.com">[gregorygutierez.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gregorygutierez.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">l obversation de cussac en 1968 historique des debats</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">L&#x27;observation de Cussac en 1967: historique des débats...</span></span></span>

Yet the delay weakens the evidential force of those results. By 1978 the children were adults or approaching adulthood, and the encounter had been repeatedly reported and illustrated. The inquiry could assess their later consistency, but it could not recreate an uncontaminated first interview.

François reportedly described the object rising in five or six widening spirals and compared the sound or air movement to a motorboat starting. ### The 1983–85 counter-investigation

Thierry Pinvidic, Bertrand Méheust and Jean-Pierre Grangeon re-examined Cussac between 1983 and 1985. Their interviews complicated both the extraterrestrial reading and simple psychological explanations.

François reportedly reduced or qualified several celebrated features. He said the smell had been brief, denied seeing a physical trace and questioned the reliability of his sister’s description of webbed feet because foliage obscured the lower part of the scene. He placed the observation at about thirty seconds. Anne-Marie, interviewed separately, continued to say that she had seen structures like supports beneath the sphere.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://gregorygutierez.com/doku.php/ovni/l_obversation_de_cussac_en_1968_historique_des_debats?do=export_pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gregorygutierez.com">[gregorygutierez.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gregorygutierez.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">l obversation de cussac en 1968 historique des debats</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">L&#x27;observation de Cussac en 1967: historique des débats...</span></span></span>

The investigators also spoke to the field guard, who had heard a loud whistle from his barn and initially interpreted it as a helicopter passing overhead. The farmer who met the children confirmed their terror but had noticed no phenomenon himself because he was driving a noisy tractor in the opposite direction. ## Could the object have been a helicopter?

The most developed conventional explanation is that the children misperceived a light helicopter and its crew. Sceptical investigator Éric Maillot argued that a bubble-canopied aircraft such as an Alouette could have reflected the Sun and appeared spherical. Crew members seen against shade or vegetation might have looked uniformly black and smaller than they were. A person boarding after take-off could have seemed to rise through the air. Rotor or turbine noise could account for the whistle and rush, while exhaust could explain the smell.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://skepticalinquirer.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/29/2009/01/p47.pdf?ref=thegalacticmind.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: skepticalinquirer.org">[Skeptical Inquirer]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">skepticalinquirer.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Skeptical Inquirer</span></span></span>

The local geometry offers some support. The Sun was behind the children, potentially producing glare from a curved windscreen, while the figures and aircraft would have been viewed near a shaded hedge. At around 80 metres, size, shape and movement could be misjudged, particularly during a surprising event lasting only seconds.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://gregorygutierez.com/doku.php/ovni/l_obversation_de_cussac_en_1968_historique_des_debats?do=export_pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gregorygutierez.com">[gregorygutierez.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gregorygutierez.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">l obversation de cussac en 1968 historique des debats</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">L&#x27;observation de Cussac en 1967: historique des débats...</span></span></span>

There are also indirect reasons not to dismiss aviation too quickly. The field guard reportedly thought the sound resembled a helicopter. Later correspondence cited by researchers suggested that the gendarmes themselves had considered an aircraft of the French Army’s light aviation branch. French sceptical reviewers have criticised GEPAN for rejecting the helicopter possibility through a classification grid rather than by fully tracing aircraft movements, local units, fuel range and operational records.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://gregorygutierez.com/doku.php/ovni/l_obversation_de_cussac_en_1968_historique_des_debats?do=export_pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gregorygutierez.com">[gregorygutierez.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gregorygutierez.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">l obversation de cussac en 1968 historique des debats</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">L&#x27;observation de Cussac en 1967: historique des débats...</span></span></span>

However, the hypothesis remains unproved. No flight log, crew testimony or aircraft identification has been found. Some of Maillot’s suggested reasons for landing—balloon recovery, military activity, surveying or a brief personal stop—are possibilities rather than documented events. The children’s description of four figures entering a small sphere, followed by a brilliant spiralling departure, does not map neatly onto a helicopter unless several perceptual errors and later narrative changes occurred together.

Supporters of the case also argue that the witnesses failed to match the smell to kerosene samples during GEPAN testing, and that the claimed luminosity and ascent pattern were unlike helicopter behaviour. These objections are weakened by the eleven-year delay and by uncertainty over when the details were first recorded, but they prevent the helicopter theory from functioning as a complete debunking.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://gregorygutierez.com/doku.php/ovni/l_obversation_de_cussac_en_1968_historique_des_debats?do=export_pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gregorygutierez.com">[gregorygutierez.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gregorygutierez.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">l obversation de cussac en 1968 historique des debats</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">L&#x27;observation de Cussac en 1967: historique des débats...</span></span></span>

The sensible assessment is that a helicopter is a plausible explanatory model, not an established solution. It accounts economically for several otherwise separate features, but lacks the decisive documentary link that would close the case.

## Did contemporary UFO stories influence the report?

Another dispute concerns the cultural atmosphere of the summer of 1967. Several weeks before Cussac, newspapers had reported a story from Arc-sous-Cicon in the Doubs department involving children who claimed to see four small figures in black clothing. France had also experienced extensive reporting of a bright atmospheric re-entry associated with the Cosmos 169 launch, initially discussed as a possible UFO.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://gregorygutierez.com/doku.php/ovni/l_obversation_de_cussac_en_1968_historique_des_debats?do=export_pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gregorygutierez.com">[gregorygutierez.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gregorygutierez.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">l obversation de cussac en 1968 historique des debats</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">L&#x27;observation de Cussac en 1967: historique des débats...</span></span></span>

The resemblance to Cussac is notable: four small dark beings, child witnesses and a period of heightened public interest. Maillot argued that these stories may have influenced the children directly or shaped the adults, journalists and investigators who interpreted their account. Even an isolated village without widespread television ownership was not sealed off from radio, newspapers, visitors or word of mouth.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://gregorygutierez.com/doku.php/ovni/l_obversation_de_cussac_en_1968_historique_des_debats?do=export_pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gregorygutierez.com">[gregorygutierez.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gregorygutierez.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">l obversation de cussac en 1968 historique des debats</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">L&#x27;observation de Cussac en 1967: historique des débats...</span></span></span>

This does not show that François and Anne-Marie copied another story. There is no firm evidence that they knew of the Arc-sous-Cicon report before their own experience. Nor does cultural influence require deliberate fabrication. An ambiguous aircraft, people or reflected object could have been interpreted through ideas already circulating in the community. Later interviewers could then reinforce the more extraordinary reading through questions, artwork and published comparisons.

The alternative is that the similarity is coincidental and that the children accurately described an event unlike any conventional activity. Cussac cannot decide between these possibilities because no uncontaminated transcript of the first full questioning is available.

## Which evidence still stands up?

After stripping away uncertain embellishments, three parts of the case remain reasonably robust.

**The children reported something promptly.** The event was discussed with adults, authorities and the press within days rather than being recovered as a late-life memory. The central structure—dark figures, a bright rounded object and a sudden departure—was not invented decades later.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.zetetique.fr/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/OvniDuCnes_chapitre10.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: zetetique.fr">[Observatoire zététique]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">zetetique.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Observatoire zététique</span></span></span>

**Their fear appears genuine.** Adults encountered them in a visibly distressed state. Later disagreement concerns what frightened them, not whether they were upset. A planned hoax is possible in principle, but the available testimony does not make it the most natural explanation.

**At least one independent person heard a notable sound.** The field guard’s recollection of a loud whistle offers limited corroboration, although his own interpretation was a helicopter and his account was formally collected many years afterwards.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://gregorygutierez.com/doku.php/ovni/l_obversation_de_cussac_en_1968_historique_des_debats?do=export_pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gregorygutierez.com">[gregorygutierez.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gregorygutierez.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">l obversation de cussac en 1968 historique des debats</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">L&#x27;observation de Cussac en 1967: historique des débats...</span></span></span>

The evidence that does not stand up as strongly includes the supposed landing trace, exact dimensions, webbed feet, a handheld mirror, landing legs and the detailed spiral trajectory. These points are either poorly documented, visibility-dependent or most clearly preserved in later sources.

There is also no independent confirmation of the central extraordinary claim. No adult saw the figures or object. Nearby agricultural workers reportedly noticed nothing. There was no photograph, instrument reading, radar return, recovered fragment or contemporary flight investigation. The case therefore rests overwhelmingly on human recollection.

## What Cussac can and cannot establish

Cussac remains unresolved in the ordinary sense that no explanation has been demonstrated beyond reasonable dispute. “Unresolved”, however, is not equivalent to “evidence of extraterrestrial visitors”. It means that the surviving information cannot securely distinguish among an unusual aircraft encounter, a complex misperception, culturally shaped interpretation, an undocumented local event or a genuinely unfamiliar phenomenon.

The case is stronger than a distant anonymous light because it involved two identified witnesses, rapid reporting, visible distress and official attention. It is weaker than popular retellings suggest because the physical evidence is unverified and the richest details depend on interviews held eleven or more years later.

Later reporting both strengthened and weakened it. GEPAN’s reconstruction treated the witnesses seriously and found no ready conventional answer. The 1980s counter-investigation confirmed their continued conviction and some independent observations, but removed confidence from the landing trace and several anatomical details. Sceptical work supplied a coherent helicopter scenario and exposed gaps in the official inquiry, yet never identified the aircraft required by that explanation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.zetetique.fr/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/OvniDuCnes_chapitre10.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: zetetique.fr">[zetetique.fr]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">zetetique.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Observatoire zététique</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Observatoire zététique</span></span></span>

Within Cantal’s UFO history, that is why Cussac still dominates. It is not a case in which hard physical evidence compels an extraordinary conclusion. It is a case in which a short, frightening rural encounter became increasingly elaborate as police recollection, journalism, official investigation, UFO advocacy and sceptical criticism accumulated around two children’s original story. The most defensible conclusion is that they probably experienced something they did not understand. What that something was remains disputed, and the surviving evidence is too incomplete to settle it.
