## Introduction

The Argentan encounter of 3 August 1992 is Orne’s most spectacular recorded UFO story: a holidaying driver said that a misty oval object appeared beside his moving car, with a human-like figure visible inside, before violet rays crossed above the vehicle and a larger green, cigar-shaped object departed at extreme speed. His wife reportedly confirmed parts of the sighting. Yet the official conclusion was not “unexplained”. GEIPAN, the French space agency’s unit for unidentified aerospace phenomena, placed the case in category C because it lacked reliable information.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cnes-geipan.fr/en/node/51675" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cnes-geipan.fr">[GEIPAN]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cnes-geipan.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">GEIPANARGENTAN (61) 03.08.1992Geipan. That distinction matters. Argentan is memorable because of its detail, not because its evidence is strong. The surviving file is essentially one principal witness’s completed questionnaire, with no separate statement from his wife, no identified independent road witness, no photograph, no radar record, no physical trace and no documented technical investigation of the vehicle. The result is a vivid claim that cannot now be reconstructed or tested with confidence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cnes-geipan.fr/en/node/51675" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cnes-geipan.fr">[GEIPAN]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cnes-geipan.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">GEIPANARGENTAN (61) 03.08.1992Geipan. ## The roadside mist, oval craft and reported pilot

The principal witness said that he was driving on a departmental road at about 1 am, during the night of 2–3 August 1992. He described a long, straight road through open fields and estimated his speed at roughly 100 kilometres per hour. The sky was said to be clear and cloudless, with no wind, and the area was away from houses. His wife and three children were in the car, although the children were asleep.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cnes-geipan.fr/sites/default/files/Questionnaire%20terre-R50.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cnes-geipan.fr">[GEIPAN]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cnes-geipan.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on cnes-geipan.fr.</span></span></span>

According to his account, he first noticed a lenticular halo of mist above the roadside vegetation on his left, about ten metres from the car and around three metres above the ground. As the mist faded, he said that an oval, dark-grey object became visible. He estimated it at approximately three metres across and two metres high. Its upper part appeared transparent.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cnes-geipan.fr/sites/default/files/Questionnaire%20terre-R50.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cnes-geipan.fr">[GEIPAN]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cnes-geipan.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on cnes-geipan.fr.</span></span></span>

The most striking element was what he believed he saw inside. He described a human form in a piloting position, its body leaning slightly backwards, with one arm bent at a right angle. The figure appeared to wear a silver suit and a helmet which reminded him of those represented in images of ancient Egyptian royalty. The front of the oval reportedly ended in a rounded projection. No sound was heard.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cnes-geipan.fr/sites/default/files/Questionnaire%20terre-R50.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cnes-geipan.fr">[GEIPAN]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cnes-geipan.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on cnes-geipan.fr.</span></span></span>

This level of detail gives the encounter its narrative force, but it does not automatically make the observation precise. The witness was viewing through a car window at night while travelling quickly. Even if the object was genuinely only ten metres away, the car would cover nearly 28 metres each second at 100 kilometres per hour. Unless the phenomenon moved alongside the vehicle, the closest view may therefore have lasted only moments. The file provides no measured duration, surveyed position or exact road alignment from which the claimed distance and size can be independently checked.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cnes-geipan.fr/sites/default/files/Questionnaire%20terre-R50.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cnes-geipan.fr">[GEIPAN]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cnes-geipan.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on cnes-geipan.fr.</span></span></span>

The apparent mist is also difficult to interpret. It could have been part of the reported phenomenon, but it could equally have affected the witness’s perception of outline, transparency and depth. The questionnaire records a clear night and no general fog, which makes a small isolated patch seem unusual, but no meteorological data or site examination survives in the public file to establish what was present near the road.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cnes-geipan.fr/sites/default/files/Questionnaire%20terre-R50.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cnes-geipan.fr">[GEIPAN]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cnes-geipan.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on cnes-geipan.fr.</span></span></span>

## The second witness and the claimed high-speed departure

The driver wrote that he immediately regarded the sight as abnormal and woke his wife, asking whether she could see the same thing. She reportedly said that she could. He then flashed the car’s headlights. In response, he claimed, violet-coloured beams appeared in short bursts above the car, at a height of roughly ten metres, arranged in a triangular pattern.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cnes-geipan.fr/sites/default/files/Questionnaire%20terre-R50.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cnes-geipan.fr">[GEIPAN]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cnes-geipan.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on cnes-geipan.fr.</span></span></span>

He said that fear led him to ask his wife whether he should slow down or accelerate. She told him to accelerate. The oval object on the left then disappeared from his view. He asked her to turn around and observe through the rear window. She reportedly saw, several hundred metres above them, an elongated green object resembling a cylinder or cigar, which then vanished at very high speed. Neither witness reported hearing any sound.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cnes-geipan.fr/sites/default/files/Questionnaire%20terre-R50.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cnes-geipan.fr">[GEIPAN]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cnes-geipan.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on cnes-geipan.fr.</span></span></span>

A second observer would normally strengthen a case, but the surviving record limits that advantage. The wife did not provide a separate published questionnaire or independently timed narrative. Everything attributed to her appears within her husband’s account. She was also awakened after he had already interpreted the sight as an unidentified craft and was asked directly whether she saw the same thing. That does not invalidate her reported observation, but it means the file cannot test how much the two descriptions agreed before discussion.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cnes-geipan.fr/sites/default/files/Questionnaire%20terre-R50.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cnes-geipan.fr">[GEIPAN]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cnes-geipan.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on cnes-geipan.fr.</span></span></span>

There is also a notable difference between what each spouse allegedly saw at the end. The husband’s close object was a small grey oval containing a figure; his wife’s departing object was a green cigar or cylinder hundreds of metres overhead. They may have been two views of one phenomenon, two separate phenomena, or impressions produced under very different viewing conditions. The dossier contains no geometry, drawings from both witnesses or sequence analysis capable of choosing between those possibilities.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cnes-geipan.fr/sites/default/files/Questionnaire%20terre-R50.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cnes-geipan.fr">[GEIPAN]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cnes-geipan.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on cnes-geipan.fr.</span></span></span>

The account mentions the red rear lights of another vehicle more than a kilometre ahead. That vehicle could have provided valuable independent corroboration, particularly if violet beams or a fast-moving luminous object had crossed the road. No statement from its occupants appears in the available record, and there is no indication that they were identified. The children in the witnesses’ car were asleep and therefore added no further testimony.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cnes-geipan.fr/sites/default/files/Questionnaire%20terre-R50.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cnes-geipan.fr">[GEIPAN]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cnes-geipan.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on cnes-geipan.fr.</span></span></span>

## Why dramatic detail could not overcome missing evidence

GEIPAN’s category C is sometimes misunderstood as a lesser form of “unexplained”. It is better read as “not reliably assessable”. In its published Argentan entry, GEIPAN identifies the phenomenon as a single silent oval object, reportedly about ten metres away and three metres in diameter, but labels the case as lacking reliable information. Its classification therefore says more about the weakness of the record than about the possible nature of the object.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cnes-geipan.fr/en/node/51675" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cnes-geipan.fr">[GEIPAN]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cnes-geipan.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">GEIPANARGENTAN (61) 03.08.1992Geipan. Groupe d&#x27;études et d&#x27;informations sur les... ARGENTAN (61) 03.08.1992. Observation date. 03/08...</span></span></span>

The principal evidential gaps are substantial:

* **No independent second testimony:** the wife’s observations are relayed through the main witness rather than preserved in her own account.
* **No contemporary physical record:** there are no photographs, video recordings, radar data or authenticated audio communications.
* **No precise location in the public file:** identifying details were redacted, preventing an independent reconstruction of the road, sight lines, vegetation and possible nearby light sources.
* **No known trace investigation:** the object was said to remain above roadside plants, but the file records no damage, residue, marks, radiation measurement or site inspection.
* **No corroboration from other road users:** the vehicle visible ahead was not linked to a witness statement.
* **No firm timing:** estimates of distance, height, size and speed are given, but the public record does not establish the duration of each phase accurately enough for meaningful calculations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cnes-geipan.fr/en/node/51675" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cnes-geipan.fr">[GEIPAN]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cnes-geipan.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">GEIPANARGENTAN (61) 03.08.1992Geipan. The witness later associated the experience with electrical faults in his car. He wrote that the radio showed no interference during the sighting, but that relay problems began several weeks afterwards, followed by starting and flow-meter faults which mechanics reportedly failed to solve. The car was eventually abandoned. This sounds suggestive when added to the encounter, but the delay is a serious weakness: no fault occurred during the observation, and the file contains no repair invoices, diagnostic reports or expert finding connecting the later failures to an external electromagnetic effect.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cnes-geipan.fr/sites/default/files/Questionnaire%20terre-R50.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cnes-geipan.fr">[GEIPAN]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cnes-geipan.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on cnes-geipan.fr.</span></span></span>

Witness character also cannot replace corroboration. The questionnaire records the principal witness as a 35-year-old social-sector professional with post-secondary qualifications, and his wife as 39. Those details may help investigators understand the observer, but occupation, education and apparent sincerity cannot prove that estimates of distance, shape or speed were accurate. Honest witnesses can misperceive brief events, particularly at night, from moving vehicles and after a surprise stimulus.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cnes-geipan.fr/sites/default/files/Questionnaire%20terre-R50.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cnes-geipan.fr">[GEIPAN]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cnes-geipan.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on cnes-geipan.fr.</span></span></span>

Nor does the weakness of the evidence establish a conventional explanation. The surviving material is too limited to show that the witnesses saw an aircraft, reflection, roadside structure, localised haze or some combination of lights and movement. The important conclusion is narrower: no extraordinary interpretation can be supported because the basic observational record is not strong enough to discriminate between competing possibilities.

## How later reporting affected the case

Later digitisation made the original questionnaire publicly accessible and preserved details that might otherwise have been reduced to folklore. It confirmed that the story was not merely an internet retelling: a formal case file exists, and its central elements—mist, oval object, apparent pilot, violet rays and rapid departure—are present in the witness’s own written narrative.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cnes-geipan.fr/sites/default/files/Questionnaire%20terre-R50.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cnes-geipan.fr">[GEIPAN]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cnes-geipan.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on cnes-geipan.fr.</span></span></span>

At the same time, publication weakened the case as evidence because readers could see how little independent material accompanied the extraordinary account. GEIPAN’s current database lists one testimony and one questionnaire, classifies the sighting C, and explicitly describes the problem as a lack of reliable information. A secondary public database based on GEIPAN records likewise lists Argentan as a single-witness category C case rather than an unresolved category D event.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cnes-geipan.fr/en/node/51675" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cnes-geipan.fr">[cnes-geipan.fr]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cnes-geipan.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">GEIPANARGENTAN (61) 03.08.1992Geipan. This makes Argentan an instructive contrast with Orne cases such as Aunay-les-Bois in 2020, where several witnesses were interviewed separately, investigators examined viewing geometry, visited the site and tested specific conventional hypotheses. Argentan is stranger in content but much weaker in investigative substance. Its story has not been convincingly debunked, yet neither has later reporting supplied the missing records needed to strengthen it.

The fairest assessment is therefore that Argentan remains an unusually elaborate personal encounter claim, not a securely unresolved aerospace event. Its place in Orne’s UFO history rests on the vividness of the testimony and the caution of the official classification: spectacular description, fragile corroboration and no firm evidential route to a conclusion.

    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
