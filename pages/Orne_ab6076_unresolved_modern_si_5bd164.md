## Introduction

Orne’s two strongest modern UFO cases remain unresolved for a straightforward reason: investigators found enough detail to reject several familiar explanations, but not enough objective evidence to identify what the witnesses saw. At Aunay-les-Bois in June 2020, three adults reported two bluish lights crossing the rural night sky in silence. GEIPAN, the French national space agency’s specialist investigation group, conducted interviews, visited the site and tested meteor, balloon and drone hypotheses before retaining an unexplained classification.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.geipan.fr/en/node/61216?field_classification_des_cas_target_id%5B0%5D=14&amp;page=%2C0" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: geipan.fr">[Geipan]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">geipan.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AUNAY-LES-BOIS (61) 03.06.2020 &#124; GEIPAN...</span></span></span>



<img src="{{ "/assets/images/Orne_ab6076_unresolved_modern_si_5bd164-overview.webp" | relative_url }}" alt="Overview image for Modern Cases" loading="eager" decoding="sync" fetchpriority="high">

The May 2013 Ménil-Hubert-sur-Orne sighting is weaker but still significant. A lone motorist described a dark, pebble-shaped phenomenon with red lights and a high-pitched hum. Investigators found no obvious conventional match, yet the absence of photographs, additional witnesses or measurable traces sharply limits what can be concluded. Together, the cases illustrate the most important distinction in Orne’s modern record: “unexplained” means that no tested explanation fitted the available testimony, not that an extraordinary craft was established.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://geipan.fr/fr/cas/2013-05-08459?field_date_value=2004-04-23&amp;field_is_new_value=All&amp;field_is_revisited_value=All&amp;field_type_de_cas_target_id=All&amp;order=field_date_d_observation&amp;page=61&amp;sort=desc" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: geipan.fr">[Geipan]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">geipan.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">MENIL-HUBERT-SUR-ORNE (61) 29.05.2013 &#124; GEIPAN...</span></span></span>

## The Aunay-les-Bois sighting and its three witnesses

Between midnight and 1 am on 3 June 2020, three adults were spending the evening around a fire in the garden of a family house in the countryside at Aunay-les-Bois. They saw a phenomenon dominated by two similarly bright bluish lights, apparently accompanied by a halo or smaller points of light. It moved horizontally at a steady speed, roughly from the south-south-east towards the east, and remained visible for an estimated five to ten seconds. No sound was reported.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.geipan.fr/en/node/61216?field_classification_des_cas_target_id%5B0%5D=14&amp;page=%2C0" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: geipan.fr">[Geipan]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">geipan.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AUNAY-LES-BOIS (61) 03.06.2020 &#124; GEIPAN...</span></span></span>

The viewing conditions gave investigators something more useful than a simple description of lights against an empty sky. The witnesses said the phenomenon briefly disappeared behind a row of poplar trees before continuing out of sight. Because the trees provided a known foreground reference, GEIPAN could attempt to constrain the object’s apparent path and maximum possible distance. The reported elevation was low, around 15 degrees above the horizon, while hedges restricted parts of the witnesses’ wider field of view.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.geipan.fr/en/node/61216?field_classification_des_cas_target_id%5B0%5D=14&amp;page=%2C0" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: geipan.fr">[Geipan]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">geipan.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AUNAY-LES-BOIS (61) 03.06.2020 &#124; GEIPAN...</span></span></span>

The presence of three witnesses strengthened the case, but not as much as three wholly independent accounts would have done. Each completed a technical questionnaire and produced drawings, and investigators later conducted cognitive interviews intended to recover details without relying only on a brief written statement. GEIPAN nevertheless noted signs that some questionnaire responses may have been agreed or discussed beforehand. That does not show dishonesty, but it makes similarities between the accounts less powerful as independent confirmation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.geipan.fr/en/node/61216?field_classification_des_cas_target_id%5B0%5D=14&amp;page=%2C0" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: geipan.fr">[Geipan]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">geipan.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AUNAY-LES-BOIS (61) 03.06.2020 &#124; GEIPAN...</span></span></span>

GEIPAN also carried out a site visit and examined the witnesses’ angular estimates. Its current case page gives the report a consistency score of 0.72 and a strangeness score of 0.53. In GEIPAN’s terminology, consistency concerns the quantity and reliability of the information collected, while strangeness concerns how poorly the reported features fit recognised phenomena. These figures are investigative ratings, not probabilities that the sighting was extraordinary.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.geipan.fr/en/node/61216?field_classification_des_cas_target_id%5B0%5D=14&amp;page=%2C0" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: geipan.fr">[Geipan]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">geipan.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AUNAY-LES-BOIS (61) 03.06.2020 &#124; GEIPAN...</span></span></span>

Aunay-les-Bois therefore stands above most casual light reports in Orne. It involved several witnesses, a defined route across the sky, foreground landmarks, detailed questionnaires, drawings, interviews and an on-site reconstruction. Yet it still depended almost entirely on human estimates. Nobody obtained a photograph or video, and no independent sensor fixed the lights’ distance, altitude or true speed.

That last weakness matters because apparent size and motion depend heavily on distance. Two nearby lights moving moderately fast can look similar to a much larger and faster phenomenon farther away. The witnesses’ impression was real evidence about what appeared in their field of view, but it could not by itself establish the object’s physical dimensions.

## How investigators tested meteors, balloons and drones

The Aunay-les-Bois report was not left unexplained simply because the lights looked unusual. GEIPAN tested several ordinary possibilities against the colour, direction, duration, apparent speed and viewing geometry.

**A fragmenting meteor or atmospheric entry** initially offered a reasonable comparison. Bright meteors can appear blue, develop an elongated or divided form and follow an apparently straight, shallow path. The short duration also fitted better than it would for many aircraft encounters. Investigators found no corresponding observation in the astronomical and automated-camera records they consulted, however. They argued that an atmospheric event large and bright enough to match the witnesses’ apparent estimates should probably have been recorded elsewhere.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.geipan.fr/en/node/61216?field_classification_des_cas_target_id%5B0%5D=14&amp;page=%2C0" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: geipan.fr">[Geipan]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">geipan.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AUNAY-LES-BOIS (61) 03.06.2020 &#124; GEIPAN...</span></span></span>

This is a meaningful negative finding because France had a national meteor-monitoring infrastructure by 2020. The FRIPON network began installing approximately 100 all-sky cameras in 2016 and was designed to detect bright atmospheric entries and reconstruct their trajectories through multiple observations. A missing detection cannot prove that no meteor occurred: cameras have weather, coverage and sensitivity limitations. It nevertheless weakens the meteor hypothesis when combined with the absence of corroborating public reports.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.fripon.org/history/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: fripon.org">[www.fripon.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">fripon.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">www.fripon.org History – www.fripon.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Starting from 2016, scientists from neighboring countries were interested in joining the scientific project through the use of the FRIPON...</span></span></span>

**Illuminated balloons** could reproduce multiple lights, silence and a slow passage. The problem was their expected movement. Balloons at sufficient altitude would broadly follow the prevailing wind, but GEIPAN found the wind direction poorly aligned, or incompatible, with the witnesses’ reported track. The measured wind speeds at the relevant levels were also substantially below the estimated apparent speed of the phenomenon.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.geipan.fr/en/node/61216?field_classification_des_cas_target_id%5B0%5D=14&amp;page=%2C0" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: geipan.fr">[Geipan]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">geipan.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AUNAY-LES-BOIS (61) 03.06.2020 &#124; GEIPAN...</span></span></span>

That conclusion depends on the accuracy of the reconstructed path and distance. A witness can estimate direction reasonably well when trees and buildings provide references, but estimating speed remains difficult without knowing range. The balloon explanation was therefore judged unsatisfactory rather than physically impossible under every conceivable set of assumptions.

**One or more drones** formed the most flexible remaining hypothesis. A drone a few hundred metres away could move at a compatible speed, remain comparatively quiet and carry coloured lighting. GEIPAN consulted a French Navy drone instructor, who said that blue lights may be used on military drones but would normally flash. The reported lights were not described that way. Civilian night flying was also restricted, although an unlawful flight could not be excluded merely because it would have breached regulations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.geipan.fr/en/node/61216?field_classification_des_cas_target_id%5B0%5D=14&amp;page=%2C0" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: geipan.fr">[Geipan]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">geipan.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AUNAY-LES-BOIS (61) 03.06.2020 &#124; GEIPAN...</span></span></span>

The foreground trees produced the main difficulty for a single-drone explanation. Using the witnesses’ angular estimates and the claim that the phenomenon passed behind trees about 200 metres away, GEIPAN calculated that one object joining the two principal lights would have needed to be more than nine metres across. That is far larger than a normal recreational drone.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.geipan.fr/en/node/61216?field_classification_des_cas_target_id%5B0%5D=14&amp;page=%2C0" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: geipan.fr">[Geipan]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">geipan.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AUNAY-LES-BOIS (61) 03.06.2020 &#124; GEIPAN...</span></span></span>

A formation of separate drones avoids that size problem. Two or more machines could fly with spacing that created the appearance of one broad phenomenon. GEIPAN did not call this impossible, but judged it unlikely because it required several coordinated drones, unusual lighting and a night operation for which no supporting evidence emerged.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.geipan.fr/en/node/61216?field_classification_des_cas_target_id%5B0%5D=14&amp;page=%2C0" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: geipan.fr">[Geipan]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">geipan.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AUNAY-LES-BOIS (61) 03.06.2020 &#124; GEIPAN...</span></span></span>

The important point is that each explanation failed differently. The meteor hypothesis lacked external astronomical confirmation. Balloons did not fit the reconstructed winds and speed. A single drone conflicted with the apparent geometry, while multiple drones remained technically possible but unsupported. None was eliminated with absolute certainty; none fitted well enough to become the official explanation.

## What Ménil-Hubert adds

The Ménil-Hubert-sur-Orne case occurred at about 11.45 pm on 29 May 2013. A motorist travelling on a departmental road reported seeing a dark phenomenon several tens of metres above the ground. He described it as roughly pebble-shaped, with red lights towards the front and rear. It initially appeared present in the sky, then moved slowly towards the north-west and disappeared behind a hedge. Investigators found no reason to question the witness’s sincerity. That judgement is useful but limited. Sincerity addresses whether someone appears honestly to be describing an experience; it does not establish that distance, size and shape were perceived correctly. A dark object seen at night from a moving or recently stopped vehicle can be particularly difficult to judge because there may be few reliable depth cues.

Ménil-Hubert also lacked the evidential advantages present at Aunay-les-Bois. There was one reporting witness, no photograph or video and no independent observer who could verify the direction, duration or sound. ## Where both cases remain weak

Aunay-les-Bois and Ménil-Hubert are valuable because they survived official investigation, but neither contains decisive physical evidence.

At Aunay-les-Bois, three witnesses and a site reconstruction created a comparatively good record. The main weakness is that the most important measurements still came from recollection: duration, angular separation, elevation, route, distance and speed. The passage lasted only seconds, and the witnesses did not capture imagery. Even the apparently powerful nine-metre size calculation is conditional on the lights having passed behind the particular trees at the estimated distance and on the angular measurements being reasonably accurate.

At Ménil-Hubert, the evidence is thinner. The longer observation and reported humming sound make the account interesting, but a single witness supplied every central feature. Without imagery, a second viewing position or a verified aircraft record, investigators could not test whether the perceived dark body was a real silhouette joining the lights or an interpretation created by low-light vision.

Neither case produced a landing trace, recovered material, electromagnetic effect, radar return or confirmed matching observation from another location. There is also no documented connection to a military exercise, airbase operation or aviation incident. The absence of such evidence does not disprove the sightings, but it prevents them from moving beyond unresolved witness cases.

The two reports should therefore not be treated as equal. Aunay-les-Bois is Orne’s strongest modern case because its three witnesses, viewing landmarks and extensive follow-up allowed investigators to test specific hypotheses. Ménil-Hubert is an unresolved but more fragile account whose unusual description could not be checked independently.

For Orne’s UFO history, that restrained outcome is precisely why the cases matter. Aunay-les-Bois represents a comparatively strong unresolved investigation; Ménil-Hubert represents the point at which an unusual but sincere single-witness account reaches the limits of what testimony alone can establish.

      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
